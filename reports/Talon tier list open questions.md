# Curry Powder's "V" Tier Is Deliberate, Not Data Error

Curry Powder's "V" is a real, deliberately invented row, not a typo or data error. Talon says on camera that it stands for "it varies", because curry powder blends differ by brand and recipe. Curry Powder therefore has no place on the ★/A/B/C/D/F scale, and the atlas should record it as tier "V" (varies/unrated) rather than map it to a letter. On the second question, no fan-made compilation of Talon's placements turned up in the notes. The single fan transcription found is one 0-like YouTube comment on the Grains Ranked video, posted in June 2026. It matches the CSV for all 23 grains, including Teff, Triticale, Wheat and Wild Rice. Several sources could not be fetched (Reddit, YouTube channel pages, TierMaker), so the absence of a broader compilation is not proven. The final board frames were never viewed in either video, so both answers rest on captions and comments.

## Talon invents a "V tier" and explains it aloud

The video's chapters put Curry Powder at 11:30, right after Cumin at 10:56 ([YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OrnicaRNYps)). The "A tier" heard at about 11:32 is the cumin verdict, which closes just before the curry segment. That is the likeliest source of any confusion about curry's placement. At about 12:12-12:25 the auto-captions have Talon saying that, given the variety of spices that may or may not be in curry powder and that no two combinations are the same, he will "do something completely unprecedented and put it in the V tier for it varies", adding that they will not need enough F tier for this list ([YouTube auto-captions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OrnicaRNYps)). Before that he lists typical blend components (chili, coriander, cumin, fennel seed, fenugreek, ginger, mustard seed, turmeric, black pepper) and promises to cover each separately by the end of the video.

The label is therefore intentional, and "completely unprecedented" suggests it had not appeared earlier in the series. Viewers noticed the oddity. One asked "Why is the f tier the V tier", and another asked why the curry powder icon is upside down ([YouTube comments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OrnicaRNYps)). Together these hint that the V row sits at or near the bottom of the board and that the icon may be flipped as a joke. Both are unverified without a frame. One viewer simply wrote "Curry powder: F", which misreads the placement and contradicts Talon's spoken label. The "varies" rationale is also echoed by viewers, who point out the many curry types and that some brands add MSG and salt.

## Where to file it in the atlas

Because Talon declined to rate it, the defensible record is tier "V" with a note such as "varies; unrated". Mapping it to F, as the one viewer did, would put an invented grade in the dataset that Talon never gave. The description, chapter list and conclusion chapter (25:35) never mention V or curry powder's tier, so there is no later correction or follow-up that overrides the spoken verdict. No third-party write-up of this placement was found, and only one web search plus the yt-dlp pull was run for it. Whether other episodes use V for blends or brand-variable foods (sauces, protein powders) is unchecked, so the atlas should expect that V may recur and treat it as a valid category rather than an error.

## Conclusion

The two questions have different confidence levels. The V tier is well established from Talon's own words, and the only open detail is its visual position on the board. The cross-check, by contrast, is thinner than the CSV note implies. It rests on one unliked, years-late comment, not on an independent dataset. For a series of about 30 episodes, the CSV's primary evidence should remain the videos themselves, with the comment treated as a sanity check.

Cheap next steps would close the gaps. Extract frames at about 730s and 1550s in the Herbs & Spices video to confirm V's row and the upside-down icon. Open the TierMaker pages in a browser and manually search Reddit for any fuller compilation.
